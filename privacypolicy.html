1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Content you create in the App
When you use WL Analysis to record or import a video, the App stores it together with any data you attach to it — barbell weight, tags, notes, date — and the trajectory/kinematics data it calculates from the video (bar path, velocity, acceleration, power). This data is stored locally on your device, in the App's own database and file storage. We do not upload it to our own servers — we do not operate any server that receives it — and we do not have access to it unless you choose to share it with us (for example, by attaching an exported file to a support email, or by using the in-app "Report a problem" action described in Section 3). The one case in which content leaves your device to a third party is the optional AI analysis feature described in Section 3.

If you export a video or a CSV file and then share it yourself (for example to social media or a messaging app), that file goes wherever you send it, and its handling from that point on is governed by the privacy policy of the service you shared it with, not by this notice.

If you enable automatic database backup, a copy of this local data may be included in your device's own Android backup (e.g., to your Google account), which is managed by Google under its own privacy policy, not by us.

Device permissions
The App may request access to the following device features. Each is used only for the purpose described, stays on your device, and can be revoked at any time in your device's Settings:
  • Camera — to record lifts using the App's built-in camera screen.
  • Microphone — to record audio with your videos and, if enabled in Settings, to recognize voice commands while recording. Voice commands are recognized entirely on your device by a speech model (Vosk) bundled with the App; no audio is sent anywhere for this.
  • Photos and videos / storage — to import existing recordings from your gallery and to save recordings, exported videos, and screen recordings the App produces.
  • Screen recording — when you start the App's screen recorder, Android asks you to confirm; the resulting file is saved on your device only.
  • Notifications — to let you know when a background task (import, export, or AI analysis) has finished.

Automatically collected information
We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the App is used (e.g., which screens and features are used, and how often), and Google Play Billing to process in-app purchases. These services may automatically collect certain technical information such as your device model, operating system version, app version, general (non-precise) location derived from IP address, an app-instance identifier, and usage events. This information does not include the video content you record or import. We receive purchase confirmations through Google Play Billing; we never see or receive your payment card or bank details.

Once per installation, the App also uses the Google Play Install Referrer API to record how you found the App — the referrer string Google Play provides (which may identify the campaign or link you came from) and the associated click and install timestamps — and sends it to Firebase Analytics as a single event. This tells us which channels bring people to the App; it does not identify you personally to us.

The App uses Firebase Remote Config to switch features on and off remotely (for example, to roll out or disable an experimental feature), and Firebase App Check to verify that requests to Google's AI services come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the App rather than from someone abusing our keys. Both work with an anonymous installation identifier and device/app integrity signals; neither receives your recordings or lift data.

If you opt in via the in-app consent dialog, the App also uses Sentry for automatic crash reporting. When enabled, crash reports are sent to Sentry containing technical diagnostic data: stack traces, app and operating system version, device model and device name (on Android the device name is chosen by you and may contain your name), and an anonymous installation identifier. We configure Sentry to discard the IP address of the reporting device and to omit your device's timezone and locale, so crash reports do not carry your location. Crash reporting is off by default and can be turned on or off at any time in Settings.

In the free version, Google AdMob serves advertisements. The App requests non-personalized ads only: ads are not selected on the basis of a profile built from your interests or past behavior. AdMob may still process limited information such as your device's advertising identifier, coarse location derived from IP address, and app/device data for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's Settings, and you can remove ads entirely with the ad-removal add-on.

3. AI-POWERED TECHNIQUE ANALYSIS (GEMINI)

In Short: WL Analysis includes an optional AI feature that, only when you actively request it, sends a short clip of your lift and its associated data to Google's Gemini AI (via the Firebase AI SDK) to generate a written technique analysis. It is off until you opt in through a consent dialog, it can be switched off again at any time in Settings, and no video is uploaded unless you choose to use this feature.

The first time you tap the AI analysis (or AI comparison) action on a lift — or the first time you open the purchase screen for this feature — the App shows a consent dialog explaining that continuing will upload data to Google. If you decline, nothing is sent and the feature is not used. If you accept, the App sends the following to Google's Gemini model:
  • The video clip you selected (re-encoded, scaled down, with audio removed, and limited to at most 15 seconds) — for comparisons, one clip per recording being compared. Because it shows you performing the lift, this clip may contain your face or other identifying details, and possibly those of bystanders who appear in the background.
  • The barbell trajectory data calculated by the App for that clip (bar path coordinates over time).
  • The barbell weight and tags you entered for that set, and your app language, so the response is written in the right language and with the right context.
This data is processed by Google to generate the analysis text, which is returned to the App and saved locally on your device (attached to that lift), the same as any other lift data. The clip is prepared in temporary storage and deleted from your device as soon as it has been sent. We do not retain a copy of the uploaded clip on any system of our own, and we do not use it to train any model.

Your consent choice is remembered. Once you accept, later analyses run without asking again — we do not re-display the dialog every time. You can withdraw your consent at any time under Settings → Other → "AI analysis uploads"; once switched off, no further clips are uploaded, and the next time you use the feature you will be asked to consent again. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already took place, and it does not delete analyses already saved on your device — you can delete those yourself along with the lift they belong to.

Processing of this data by Google is subject to Google's own terms for the Gemini API and Firebase AI Logic. Google acts as our processor for generating the response, and separately retains prompts and responses for a limited period for abuse monitoring and to meet its legal obligations. We encourage you to review Google's privacy policy and the Gemini API terms for details of how Google itself handles this data.

If you believe a result is inaccurate or inappropriate, you can use the "Report a problem with this result" button shown with the analysis — this opens your own email app with a pre-filled message (containing the result text and the weight/tags used as context) addressed to us at weightliftinganalysis@gmail.com; nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email yourself.

This feature is offered as a separate optional in-app purchase. Regardless of how it is licensed or which users it is made available to, the consent and data-handling described in this section always apply. The analysis is generated by a language model, may be incomplete or wrong, and is provided for general training information only — it is not coaching, medical, or health advice, and you should not rely on it as such.

6. WHERE IS YOUR INFORMATION PROCESSED?

Data that leaves your device (a clip you send to the AI feature, analytics and install-attribution events, crash reports if you enabled them, and purchase records) is processed by Google and Sentry on their own infrastructure, which is global and includes servers in the United States. This means your information may be transferred outside the European Economic Area to countries whose data protection laws differ from those of your country.

Where such a transfer takes place, it relies on the safeguards these providers put in place for their customers — typically the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, and/or the provider's certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. These providers publish their own transfer terms, and we encourage you to review them. If you would prefer that no lift video of yours is transferred in this way, simply do not enable the AI analysis feature; the rest of the App works entirely on your device.

8. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

Your recordings, lift data, tags, and AI analysis results are stored locally on your device and remain there until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App — we do not control or set a retention period for this local data, since we never receive a copy of it.

For data held by our providers: the clip you send to the AI feature is deleted from your device immediately after it is sent, and is retained by Google only for the limited period described in its Gemini API terms (principally for abuse monitoring); analytics and install-attribution events are retained by Firebase according to the retention period configured for our project and Google's own policies; crash reports are retained by Sentry for the retention period of our plan (by default around 90 days) and can be deleted sooner on request; purchase records are retained by Google Play, and by us for as long as we are required to keep transaction records for tax and accounting purposes. Data you send us directly (a support email or a "Report a problem" message) is kept only as long as needed to address your request and to keep a record of the issue, and is then deleted.

9. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

Because most of your data stays on your own device, its security depends significantly on your device's own security (e.g., screen lock, OS updates). Data sent to the AI feature, analytics, crash reporting, or billing providers is transmitted using industry-standard encryption in transit, and access to our provider dashboards is protected by strong authentication. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
