1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Content you create in the App
When you use WL Analysis to record or import a video, the App stores it together with any data you attach to it — barbell weight, tags, notes, date — and the trajectory/kinematics data it calculates from the video (bar path, velocity, acceleration, power). This data is stored locally on your device, in the App's own database and file storage. We do not upload it to our own servers, and we do not have access to it unless you choose to share it with us (for example, by attaching an exported file to a support email, or by using the in-app "Report a problem" action described in Section 3).

If you enable automatic database backup, a copy of this local data may be included in your device's own Android backup (e.g., to your Google account), which is managed by Google under its own privacy policy, not by us.

Device permissions
The App may request access to the following device features. Each is used only for the purpose described, stays on your device, and can be revoked at any time in your device's Settings:
  • Camera — to record lifts using the App's built-in camera screen.
  • Microphone — to record audio with your videos and, if enabled in Settings, to recognize voice commands while recording (processed on-device).
  • Photos and videos / storage — to import existing recordings from your gallery and to save recordings, exported videos, and screen recordings the App produces.
  • Notifications — to let you know when a background task (import, export, or AI analysis) has finished.

Automatically collected information
We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the App is used (e.g., which screens and features are used, and how often), and Google Play Billing to process in-app purchases. These services may automatically collect certain technical information such as your device model, operating system version, app version, general (non-precise) location derived from IP address, and usage events. This information does not include the video content you record or import.

If you opt in via the in-app consent dialog, the App also uses Sentry for automatic crash reporting. When enabled, crash reports containing technical diagnostic data (device/OS info, stack traces, and an anonymous installation identifier) are sent to Sentry to help us fix bugs. This is off by default and can be turned on or off at any time in Settings.

3. AI-POWERED TECHNIQUE ANALYSIS (GEMINI)

In Short: WL Analysis includes an optional AI feature that, only when you actively request it, sends a short clip of your lift and its associated data to Google's Gemini AI (via the Firebase AI SDK) to generate a written technique analysis. This is off by default, requires your explicit consent every time it is offered, and no video is uploaded unless you choose to use this feature.

When you tap the AI analysis (or AI comparison) action on a lift, the App shows a consent dialog explaining that continuing will upload data to Google. If you decline, nothing is sent and the feature is not used. If you accept, the App sends the following to Google's Gemini model:
  • The video clip you selected (re-encoded, with audio removed, capped at roughly 15 seconds) — for comparisons, one clip per recording being compared. Because it shows you performing the lift, this clip may contain your face or other identifying details, and possibly those of bystanders who appear in the background.
  • The barbell trajectory data calculated by the App for that clip (bar path coordinates over time).
  • The barbell weight and tags you entered for that set, and your app language, so the response is written in the right language and with the right context.
This data is processed by Google to generate the analysis text, which is returned to the App and saved locally on your device (attached to that lift), the same as any other lift data. We do not otherwise retain a copy of the uploaded clip on our own systems, and it is not used by us to train any model. Processing of this data by Google is subject to Google's own privacy and data-handling terms for the Gemini API / Firebase AI Logic; we encourage you to review Google's privacy policy for details on how Google itself handles this data.

You can withdraw consent at any time by simply not using the AI analysis/comparison feature again; each use requires the same opt-in. If you believe a result is inaccurate or inappropriate, you can use the "Report a problem with this result" button shown with the analysis — this opens your own email app with a pre-filled message (containing the result text and the weight/tags used as context) addressed to us at weightliftinganalysis@gmail.com; nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email yourself.

This feature is currently offered as part of an optional Pro add-on; regardless of how it is licensed, the consent and data-handling described in this section always apply.

7. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

Your recordings, lift data, tags, and AI analysis results are stored locally on your device and remain there until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App — we do not control or set a retention period for this local data, since we never receive a copy of it. Analytics, crash reporting, billing, and AdMob data are retained by those providers according to their own retention policies; we only access this data in aggregate or as needed to operate the App. Data you send us directly (e.g., a support email or "Report a problem" message) is kept only as long as needed to address your request.

8. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

Because most of your data stays on your own device, its security depends significantly on your device's own security (e.g., screen lock, OS updates). Data sent to the AI feature, analytics, crash reporting, or billing providers is transmitted using industry-standard encryption in transit. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature. As no uniform technology standard for recognizing DNT signals exists yet, the App does not currently respond to them. You can still limit ad tracking specific to your device through your device's own advertising ID settings, and you can remove ads entirely with the ad-removal add-on.
